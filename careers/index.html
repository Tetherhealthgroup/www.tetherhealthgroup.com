Careers

Help build care that meets people where they are.

Tether Health is one app with 11 programs, from quitting smoking to heart health and healthy aging. We are looking for people who want health technology to be calmer, kinder and more private.

How we work

Four things we hold to.

  • People before metrics

    We measure ourselves by whether a tool helps someone on a hard day, not by how often it makes them open an app.

  • Privacy is the product

    No ads and no selling health data. Privacy and security shape what we build from the first sketch, not at the end.

  • Clinicians in the room

    We build with clinicians, and want their judgment shaping the content, the boundaries and the routes to real people in every program.

  • Built for everyone

    Plain language, careful design and accessibility treated as part of the work, not a checklist after it.

Who we look for

The people who make Tether Health.

  • Engineering

    Mobile, backend and health-data engineers who care about reliability, privacy and the standards that let records move safely — FHIR and SMART on FHIR among them.

  • Design and research

    Product designers, content designers and researchers who can make a difficult moment feel calmer with fewer words.

  • Clinical and behavioural science

    Clinicians, pharmacists and behavioural scientists who want to shape support for tobacco, heart health, diabetes, healthy aging and more.

  • Privacy, security and compliance

    Specialists in HIPAA, health-data security and digital health regulation who want to build it right from the start.

  • Partnerships and operations

    People who can bring health systems, payers, employers and public-health organisations alongside us.

Want to work with us?

Send your résumé or a link to your work, with a few lines about what you would bring and which area of health matters to you, to careers@tetherhealthgroup.com.

We welcome applicants of every background, identity and ability. If you need an adjustment to take part in our hiring process, tell us in your first message.
