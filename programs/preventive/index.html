Checkup

What’s due for you, in one place.

Checkup is the Tether Health program for preventive health. Most of prevention is remembering the right thing at the right time, so Checkup keeps what is due for you in one place, with plain explanations and a record you own.

Preventive health

What it is

Preventive health is about vaccines, screenings and routine checkups that can catch problems early or stop them starting. What is recommended depends on your age, sex, health history and other risk factors.

Common reasons people fall behind include cost, not having time, feeling nervous, not knowing where to go, and simply forgetting.

In the app

What the app helps with

  • A due list in one place

    Vaccines and screenings many adults need, grouped by what to do next. It is general guidance, not a diagnosis; your clinician says what fits you.

  • Item detail

    For each item, the options there are, a note on cost, and the public guidance it comes from.

  • Mark done

    Record what you had and when, and note when you'd like your next check.

  • Screening-day prep

    The next appointment you entered, and what to expect on the day.

Catching up

If you go off plan: the recovery plan

The catching up screen opens with “Behind on screenings? Many people are.” These are its steps.

  1. 1

    What got in the way? (optional)

    Cost, time, nervous about it, not sure where to go, or forgot.

  2. 2

    See my options for one screening

    The recommended next step: pick one to start.

  3. 3

    Ask my care team which to do first

    Shown when you have connected a care team. Or go back to your list.

  4. 4

    Save and take one step

    Nothing on your list is lost.

If cost was the reason, the screen notes that screening is often covered with no cost-sharing and suggests checking with your insurer.

What it includes

The areas Checkup covers.

Every part of Checkup follows the same principles as the rest of Tether Health: private by design, no judgment, and a route to a real person.

  • Reminders

    Timely, private prompts for the checks a person is due, with notification copy that gives nothing away on a lock screen.

  • Education

    Why a given check exists and what it involves, in plain language.

  • Age- and risk-appropriate screening

    Guidance shaped to the person rather than a generic checklist.

  • A record the person owns

    One place to see what has been done and what is due, exportable and deletable by the person it describes.
