SSocial media and endless scrolling

ScrollFree is the Tether Health program for social media use. It starts from what you say about your own use, then turns that into goals that feel doable, a pause when the urge hits, and an easy way back after a long scroll.

Social media use

What it is

Social media and endless scrolling are designed to hold attention, and it is easy to spend more time than you meant to. Heavy use can crowd out sleep, time with people and other things you care about.

Common pulls include boredom, stress, trouble sleeping, notifications, and simply not noticing the time pass.

In the app

What the app helps with

  • Self-report first

    It starts from what you say about your own use and what pulls you in.

  • Goal and app-free windows

    A daily minute goal that feels doable and the phone-free times you choose, such as a bedtime window.

  • Pause 60

    A 60-second pause for the moment the urge to scroll hits, with something else to try and no judgment if you open the app anyway.

  • Buddy and weekly reflection

    An optional accountability buddy who sees only what you choose to send, and a weekly look back at how the week went.

Long-scroll recovery

If you go off plan: the recovery plan

The long-scroll recovery screen opens with “That happens. What pulled you in?” These are its steps.

  1. 1

    What pulled you in?

    Pick a reason: couldn't sleep, bored, stressed or didn't notice time.

  2. 2

    Keep my goal, try again tonight

    The recommended next step keeps the goal you set.

  3. 3

    Make my goal easier or change my bedtime window

    Either option takes you to your goal screen to adjust it.

  4. 4

    Save

    Your phone-free nights still count.

Nothing resets after a long scroll.

What it includes

The areas ScrollFree covers.

Every part of ScrollFree follows the same principles as the rest of Tether Health: private by design, no judgment, and a route to a real person.

  • Self-report first

    Starting from what a person tells it about their own use and triggers.

  • Minute goals and app-free windows

    A daily minute goal and app-free windows the person chooses, set at a level that feels doable.

  • Pause 60

    A 60-second pause for the moment the urge to scroll hits, with the shared Rescue one tap away.

  • Buddy and weekly reflection

    An optional accountability buddy, and a weekly reflection on how the week went, without judgment after a long scroll.

  • Unplug

    Coming: optional app limits (needs Apple approval). Not part of the app yet.
