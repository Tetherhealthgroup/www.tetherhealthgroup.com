Healthy aging

What it is

Healthy aging is about staying active, steady and independent for longer. Falls are a common concern as people get older, and losing strength and balance can make everyday tasks harder.

Common contributing factors include muscle weakness, balance problems, some medicines, vision changes, long periods of sitting, and hazards at home such as loose rugs or poor lighting.

In the app

What the app helps with

  • Falls check

    A short check at the start. A fall plus feeling unsteady is added to your list of things to mention to your clinician.

  • Weekly movement plan

    Short sessions on the days you choose, played one move at a time with a countdown.

  • I fell

    Asks whether you are hurt, routes to emergency help, shows how to get up safely, and lets you log the fall.

  • Home safety and caregiver access

    A home safety checklist, and access for one person you choose, limited to Stride and removable at any time.

Gentle restart

If you go off plan: the recovery plan

The gentle restart screen opens with “After a fall or a missed week, start gently.” These are its steps.

  1. 1

    What happened? (optional)

    I had a fall, missed sessions, felt unwell, felt unsteady, or busy or away.

  2. 2

    Do my next session

    The recommended next step: near a sturdy chair, at your own pace.

  3. 3

    Go over my home safety list or talk to my clinician first

    Talking to your clinician is shown when you have a care team.

  4. 4

    Save and take one step

    Sessions you did still count.

After a fall, the screen asks you to check with your clinician before you restart exercise. Hurt, a hit to the head, or not being able to get up leads to getting help now.

What it includes

The areas Stride covers.

Every part of Stride follows the same principles as the rest of Tether Health: private by design, no judgment, and a route to a real person.

  • Prevention

    Support for the things that keep people independent longer, particularly falls.

  • Monitoring

    Light monitoring that a person or a family member can act on, without turning a home into a ward.

  • Exercise and support programs

    Structured activity and support built for the range of ability this area actually covers.

  • Supporter involvement

    Letting a family member or carer help without taking the person's decisions away from them.

Tether Health supports everyday self-care and does not diagnose, treat or replace your clinician.
