Respiratory health

What it is

Respiratory health covers long-term lung conditions such as asthma and COPD (chronic obstructive pulmonary disease), where breathing can be harder on some days than others.

Common contributing factors include smoking and secondhand smoke, air pollution, dust and fumes at work, respiratory infections, and allergens. Many people work from a written action plan agreed with their clinician.

In the app

What the app helps with

  • Your clinician's action plan

    Copy in the plan your clinician gave you. ClearAir never creates or changes zones; without a plan you can still learn, log symptoms and prepare questions.

  • Zone check

    Enter peak flow if you use it and how you feel. The zone comes only from your plan, and “can't speak in full sentences” always opens your red-zone plan.

  • Yellow- and red-zone steps

    Your own yellow-zone steps with a re-check, and a red-zone plan that is quick to open and stays open until you close it.

  • Symptom log and progress

    A light symptom log, and progress against your own zones with notes worth raising at your next visit.

Missed checks

If you go off plan: the recovery plan

The missed checks screen opens with “Missed a few days? Pick up from today.” These are its steps.

  1. 1

    What happened? (optional)

    Missed controller doses, missed peak-flow checks, ran out of medicine, felt fine so I stopped, or busy or away.

  2. 2

    Check my zone now

    The recommended next step: start again from today.

  3. 3

    Ask my care team about doses or refills

    Shown when you have connected a care team. You can also go back to your daily routine.

  4. 4

    Save and take one step

    Your earlier zone checks still count.

ClearAir never changes your medicines or doses; it asks you to check with your clinician or pharmacist about missed doses. Getting worse leads straight to your red-zone plan.

What it includes

The areas ClearAir covers.

Every part of ClearAir follows the same principles as the rest of Tether Health: private by design, no judgment, and a route to a real person.

  • Trigger and risk reduction

    Identifying and reducing the exposures that drive symptoms, connected to the tobacco work.

  • Symptom monitoring

    Light-touch symptom tracking that is useful to a clinician and not a burden to the person.

  • Care coordination

    Helping a person keep a care team informed, with nothing shared unless they choose to share it.

  • Education and self-management

    Reviewed, plain-language material on managing a long-term respiratory condition day to day.

Tether Health supports everyday self-care and does not diagnose, treat or replace your clinician.
