The programs

11 programs. One app.

Tether Health is one app with a program for each area of health. Behaviour change looks different across conditions, but the machinery underneath is shared: a plan you own, help in the moment, progress without punishment, and a route to a person. Each program shapes that to its own area — starting with BreatheFree, for tobacco and nicotine.

How we choose an area

Need, evidence, and whether we can do it honestly.

Where the burden is largest

We start where the number of people affected and the severity of outcomes are both high, and where self-management genuinely changes the picture.

Where the evidence is settled

We build on established clinical guidance, not on a promising study. If the evidence for an intervention is thin, a nicely designed app does not fix that.

Where software is the right tool

Some problems need a clinician, a prescription or a policy, not a phone. We would rather route someone to the right help than pretend an app is it.

Help us test the Tether Health app

Try test versions of the app and tell us what works. Ask for early access and we will email you an invite when there is a spot.
