Cardiovascular health

What it is

Cardiovascular health is about the heart and blood vessels, including high blood pressure, heart disease and stroke. High blood pressure often has no symptoms, which is why regular checks matter.

Common contributing factors include smoking, a diet high in salt, low physical activity, stress, diabetes, high cholesterol, and family history.

In the app

What the app helps with

  • Your clinician's targets

    Copy in the targets from your visit. Only your clinician sets targets; Heartwise never sets or grades them.

  • Two-reading blood pressure log

    Sit quietly first, take two readings a minute apart, and the app averages them, with a check on numbers that look mistyped.

  • Crisis-range route

    A very high reading asks about symptoms such as chest pain or trouble speaking, routes to 911 when they are present, and otherwise offers a timed re-check.

  • Trends, cholesterol and visit summary

    Trends over time, a cholesterol panel you enter, and a visit summary you preview before anything is sent to your clinic.

Missed or high readings

If you go off plan: the recovery plan

The missed or high readings screen opens with “Missed readings or a few high ones? Start again today.” These are its steps.

  1. 1

    What happened? (optional)

    Missed readings, several high readings, ran out of medicine, monitor problems, or busy or away.

  2. 2

    Take a reading now

    The recommended next step, starting with five quiet minutes.

  3. 3

    Message my care team or send a visit summary

    Shown when you have a care team or a clinic set up.

  4. 4

    Save and take one step

    Your earlier readings are still here.

Heartwise never changes your medicines or targets. Several readings above target may be worth telling your clinician, and chest pain, pressure or trouble breathing means calling 911.

What it includes

The areas Heartwise covers.

Every part of Heartwise follows the same principles as the rest of Tether Health: private by design, no judgment, and a route to a real person.

  • Risk assessment

    Helping a person understand their own risk factors and what changes them, without presenting it as a clinical assessment.

  • Diet and activity support

    The same behaviour-change machinery as the other areas, shaped to what this one needs.

  • Monitoring and prevention

    Support for readings a person takes at home, and clear guidance on when a number means calling someone rather than logging it.

  • Care-team coordination

    Letting a person hand readings and history to a clinician when they choose to, and never by default.

Tether Health supports everyday self-care and does not diagnose, treat or replace your clinician.
