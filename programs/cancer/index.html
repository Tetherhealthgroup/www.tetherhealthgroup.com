Cancer

What it is

Cancer is a group of diseases in which abnormal cells grow out of control. It affects people at every stage: lowering risk, going through screening and treatment, life after treatment, and caring for someone else.

Common contributing factors include tobacco use, alcohol, too much sun, carrying extra weight, low physical activity, some infections, getting older, and family history. Some risks can be changed and some cannot.

In the app

What the app helps with

  • Four journeys

    Choose the one that fits: lowering risk, in treatment, after treatment, or caring for someone. You can change it at any time.

  • Treatment diary

    A quick daily diary of how you feel, with a fever rule that says to call your oncology team now, and the care-team number you saved.

  • Visit questions

    Write down questions for your care team before an appointment.

  • Survivorship plan

    What to ask your care team for after treatment, kept where you can find it.

Rough patch

If you go off plan: the recovery plan

The rough patch screen opens with “Side effects or a missed diary? That’s okay.” These are its steps.

  1. 1

    What happened? (optional)

    Side effects got worse, missed my diary, missed an appointment, feeling low, or too tired.

  2. 2

    Fill in today's diary

    The recommended next step: about a minute.

  3. 3

    Add questions for my care team or get support for how I feel

    Support for how you feel opens the tools in Ease.

  4. 4

    Save and take one step

    Your earlier entries are still here.

A fever of 100.4°F (38°C) or higher during chemotherapy means calling your oncology team now, day or night. Severe symptoms or an emergency mean calling 911.

What it includes

The areas Beacon covers.

Every part of Beacon follows the same principles as the rest of Tether Health: private by design, no judgment, and a route to a real person.

  • Risk reduction

    Information on modifiable risk, connected to the tobacco and nutrition work rather than duplicating it.

  • Screening awareness

    What screening exists, who it is offered to, and how to raise it with a clinician. Awareness only — we do not screen.

  • Treatment-support resources

    Practical, reviewed material for people in treatment and the people supporting them.

  • Survivorship

    Support for the period after treatment, which is long, under-served, and rarely designed for.

Tether Health supports everyday self-care and does not diagnose, treat or replace your clinician.
