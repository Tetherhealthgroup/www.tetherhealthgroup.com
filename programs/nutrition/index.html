Obesity & nutrition

What it is

Obesity and nutrition cover the everyday food and activity patterns that shape long-term health. Weight is influenced by many things beyond willpower, and shame tends to make change harder, not easier.

Common contributing factors include genetics, sleep, stress, some medicines, the cost and availability of healthy food, time to cook, and how much daily movement a routine allows.

In the app

What the app helps with

  • One weekly habit

    Pick one small habit a week, such as adding a vegetable to dinner, and mark it done with one tap.

  • Weight-neutral by default

    Nourish never asks for your weight unless you turn weight tracking on, and it can stay off.

  • Protective mode

    If eating has been a struggle, Nourish does not set food amounts or show before-and-after comparisons, and offers support options, including help preparing what to say to a clinician.

  • Off-track reset and week 4 milestone

    A reset without guilt after a busy week, and a check-in after four weeks of small habits.

Gentle restart

If you go off plan: the recovery plan

The gentle restart screen opens with “Start again, small.” These are its steps.

  1. 1

    What got in the way? (optional)

    Busy, stress, felt unwell, lost interest, or prefer not to say.

  2. 2

    Restart my habit today

    The recommended next step.

  3. 3

    Pick a smaller habit or get support with food

    Or talk to someone: your care team or a person in Support.

  4. 4

    Save and take one step

    Days away don't undo the ones you did.

Nothing resets, and weight is never asked.

What it includes

The areas Nourish covers.

Every part of Nourish follows the same principles as the rest of Tether Health: private by design, no judgment, and a route to a real person.

  • Nutrition education

    Practical, non-prescriptive education that does not depend on tracking every meal.

  • Healthy-weight programs

    Structured support built around what a person can sustain, with no judgment when a week goes badly.

  • Physical activity

    Activity support that starts from what the person can do now, not from a target.

  • Support between appointments

    The long stretches where nothing is scheduled and most of the difficulty actually sits.

Tether Health supports everyday self-care and does not diagnose, treat or replace your clinician.
