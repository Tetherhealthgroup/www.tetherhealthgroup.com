For clinicians and care teams

Support between visits, inside clear boundaries.

BreatheFree is designed to do the work that does not fit into an appointment — the craving at midnight, the check-in on day nine, the week after a slip — without ever stepping into your role.

Talk to us about clinical inputReview every screen

The boundary

What it does, and what it refuses to.

The most important design decisions in BreatheFree are the things it will not do. These are constraints in the product, not disclaimers in a footer.

  • Educates on quit-smoking medicinesbutNever selects a medicine or changes a dose
  • Records what the patient reportsbutDoes not confirm how a medicine was actually used
  • Tracks self-rated craving intensitybutDoes not present ratings as clinical measurements
  • Routes to quitlines, coaches and care teamsbutSends nothing until the patient previews and confirms

Between visits

What your patient actually gets.

  • A tool for the craving itselfRate intensity on a 1–10 scale, receive one recommended coping tool matched to the trigger and to what has worked before, run it, then recheck. Offline, one-handed, no typing.
  • A plan in the patient's own wordsTheir quit path, their date, their reasons, and one named supporter with an agreed phrase. Reviewed in full before it is activated.
  • A history a slip does not eraseSlips are recorded as events within the history, with prior progress, lessons and coping practice explicitly retained. Missing check-ins are never counted as zero.
  • Reviewed, plain-language educationShort lessons on cravings, withdrawal, sleep, medicines and support — in English and Spanish, with audio and transcripts.

Consent and sharing

Nothing reaches you by accident.

Care-team sharing in BreatheFree is manual by default. The patient previews exactly what would be sent and confirms it before anything leaves the device. Consent is reviewable and revocable, and revocation is enforced rather than merely recorded.

That is deliberate. A tool that quietly forwards a patient's smoking status to their chart is a tool patients learn to lie to.

Get involved

We would rather hear your objections now.

BreatheFree is in development. Clinical review of medication, withdrawal, pregnancy and urgent-symptom content is a release gate we have not yet passed — which makes this the useful moment to tell us what is wrong.

Review our content

Look at how we explain medicines, withdrawal and slips, and tell us where the language is unsafe, unclear or out of date.

Shape the roadmap

Tell us which of the health areas we have listed matters most in your setting, and what would have to be true for you to recommend it.

Challenge the boundaries

If you think any part of the product edges toward diagnosing, treating or prescribing, we want to know before anyone uses it.

Write to us at support@tetherhealthgroup.com, or use the early-access form and tell us you are a clinician.
