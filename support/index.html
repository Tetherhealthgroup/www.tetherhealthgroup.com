Questions about the Tether Health app

What is Tether Health?

Tether Health is one app with 11 programs, one for each area of health. BreatheFree, for tobacco and nicotine, is one of them. Seeall 11 programs and where each one stands.

How do I get help with the app?

Email support@tetherhealthgroup.com. It helps to include the app version, shown at the bottom of Account. Please do not include medical details.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?" and enter your email address. If an account exists for that address, a reset link is sent to it. If you signed in with Apple or Google, tap "Continue with Apple" or "Continue with Google" instead.

How do I get a copy of my data?

In the app, open Account, then "Your data", and choose an export.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, open Account, then "Your data", then "Delete account and all data". The full steps, including what to do if you cannot sign in, are on the account deletion page.

How is my information handled?

No ads, and we never sell your health data. The details are in the Tether Health app privacy policy.

Is the app medical advice?

No. The app does not diagnose, treat or prescribe, and it does not replace your clinician. If you need help right now, use the numbers at the top of this page.

Questions about BreatheFree

How do I get BreatheFree?

BreatheFree opens to early-access members first, on Android. Request early access with the form below and we will write to you as places become available.

How do I join the testing list?

Get early access. Tell us which phone you use and we will email you an invite when there is a spot. We cannot promise a date, and joining does not guarantee a place.

What about other platforms and the other health areas?

Android comes first. When you request early access, tell us which of the 11 programs matters most to you.
