Screen tour

All 28 screens, in order.

Every BreatheFree screen, grouped into the eight phases of the journey — from the first welcome to settings and privacy.

Phase 1 of 8

Welcome

A private, judgment-free opening that asks for nothing before it gives something.

  • Screen 1

    Welcome

    Introduces Tether Health, language choice and the private, judgment-free promise.

Phase 2 of 8

Onboarding

Informed consent in plain language, then a picture of today's smoking and its triggers — recorded without judgment.

  • Screen 2

    Why Tether Health

    Explains how the app supports cravings, planning and progress.

  • Screen 3

    Consent & privacy

    Collects informed consent and explains data choices in plain language.

  • Screen 4

    Baseline assessment

    Records current smoking patterns without judgment.

  • Screen 5

    Trigger map

    Identifies situations and feelings linked to smoking.

  • Screen 6

    Readiness result

    Reflects readiness and recommends a flexible next step.

Phase 3 of 8

Quit plan

A plan the person builds themselves: a path, a date, their own reasons, and one named supporter.

  • Screen 7

    Choose quit path

    Lets the patient choose a quit-date or gradual-reduction path.

  • Screen 8

    Select quit date

    Selects a realistic quit date with planning guidance.

  • Screen 9

    My reasons

    Captures personal reasons the patient wants to quit.

  • Screen 10

    Support preparation

    Chooses a supporter and a specific help phrase.

  • Screen 11

    Review quit plan

    Reviews every plan choice before activation.

Phase 4 of 8

Daily support

A check-in that takes under ninety seconds, and one manageable next step chosen from the answer.

  • Screen 12

    Preparation home

    Daily home before quit day with tasks, check-in and craving support.

  • Screen 13

    Daily check-in

    Records a quick, nonpunitive daily status in under 90 seconds.

  • Screen 14

    Personalized next step

    Recommends one manageable action based on the check-in.

  • Screen 15

    Guided stress reset

    Runs a calm, guided breathing exercise.

  • Screen 16

    Exercise complete & recheck

    Measures how the exercise felt and recommends what to do next.

Phase 5 of 8

Craving rescue

Immediate, one-handed help, followed by an honest recheck of how it went.

  • Screen 17

    Craving rescue start

    Starts immediate, one-handed help.

  • Screen 18

    Recommended rescue tool

    Suggests a short coping tool matched to the craving.

  • Screen 19

    Active craving rescue

    Guides the selected coping tool in real time.

  • Screen 20

    Craving recheck

    Rechecks craving intensity without claiming a clinical measurement.

  • Screen 21

    Rescue result & next step

    Shows the patient-reported change and offers another safe option.

Phase 6 of 8

Quit day

Support through the hardest day, and a slip response that keeps every piece of prior progress.

  • Screen 22

    Quit-day home

    Supports the patient through quit day with immediate help and check-ins.

  • Screen 23

    Slip recovery

    Responds to a slip with support while preserving all prior progress.

Phase 7 of 8

Resources

Medication education without prescribing, plain-language lessons awaiting clinical review, patterns without punishment, and real human support.

  • Screen 24

    Medication center

    Provides education and self-tracking without prescribing.

  • Screen 25

    Learn library

    Plain-language learning resources, pending clinical review.

  • Screen 26

    Progress dashboard

    Shows patterns and estimates without punishment after slips.

  • Screen 27

    Support hub

    Connects the patient to quitlines, supporters, coaches and care teams.

Phase 8 of 8

Account

Notification privacy, consent, export, accessibility and deletion — in one place, under the person's control.

  • Screen 28

    Settings & privacy

    Centralizes notification privacy, consent, exports, accessibility and deletion.

That is the whole product.

No hidden upsell screens, no advertising slots, no data-broker handoff. What you have just walked through is what BreatheFree is.
