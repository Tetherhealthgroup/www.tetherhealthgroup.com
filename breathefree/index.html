BreatheFree

Your next breath can be different.

Small steps, practical support, and no judgment — whenever you are ready. BreatheFree supports people through quitting smoking, from the first honest look at a habit to the craving that arrives at 11pm on a Tuesday.

  • Help when cravings hitOne tap, no typing
  • Progress that staysA slip never erases your wins
Get early accessHow it works

What it does

Four things, each built for the moment it is needed.

Craving rescue

One tap opens immediate, one-handed help. Rate the craving, get one recommended tool, run it, then recheck.

A plan you build

Choose a quit-date or gradual-reduction path, pick a realistic date, write down your own reasons, and name one supporter with a phrase they will actually understand.

Progress without punishment

Smoke-free time, cigarettes avoided, money saved, and which coping tool has helped most. A slip is recorded as an event in the history, never as a reset to zero.

A route to a person

Quitlines in English and Spanish, your named supporter, a quit coach, and your care team. Nothing is sent anywhere until you preview it and confirm.

Safeguards

The boundaries we designed in first.

A tool that touches someone's health has to be careful about what it claims and what it exposes. These constraints came before the features.

  • Sensitive notifications off by default

    A lock screen shows "You have a BreatheFree reminder" — not your quit status or your medication.

  • Education, never prescribing

    The medication centre explains options and lets you record your own plan. It never selects a medicine or changes a dose.

  • Reports, not measurements

    Craving ratings describe how someone felt. The app is explicit that these are self-reports, not clinical measurements.

  • Export and deletion

    Your data can be exported and deleted, with the deletion actually completed rather than merely hidden.

Unplug

The phone is often the thing standing next to the habit.

Unplug is a module inside BreatheFree for the screen habits that sit alongside a quit attempt — the scroll at 11pm, the app opened before the thought finishes. It is designed to pause the apps you ask it to slow down and answer the reflex with your own reasons, in your own words.

  • Designed around what a phone will share

    Phones tell an app very little about how you use other apps. Unplug is designed to that limit: to pause what you ask it to, and to report only what the platform genuinely shares, rather than guessing.

  • An intercept that asks, not scolds

    When you open something you asked us to slow down, you get a pause, your own reason back in your own words, and a way through it. A breath, a sentence typed out, or a short puzzle — enough friction to interrupt a reflex.

  • Quick to tighten, slow to loosen

    Raising a limit takes one tap. Lowering one costs something every time: a wait of a day if you are on your own, or a conversation with your clinician or coach if you are not.

  • Distress reaches a qualified person

    If the same difficult feeling keeps coming up, that never comes to rest with a non-clinical coach. Where it goes instead is written down per programme by clinicians, not decided by the app.
