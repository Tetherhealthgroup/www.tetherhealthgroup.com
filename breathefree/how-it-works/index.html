How it works

Eight phases, from first look to long haul.

BreatheFree is 28 screens grouped into eight phases. Each one answers a different question, and nobody is asked to do the later ones before they are ready.

Phase 1 of 8 · 1 screen

Welcome

A private, judgment-free opening that asks for nothing before it gives something.

  1. 1WelcomeIntroduces BreatheFree, language choice and the private, judgment-free promise.

Phase 2 of 8 · 5 screens

Onboarding

Informed consent in plain language, then a picture of today's smoking and its triggers — recorded without judgment.

  1. 2Why BreatheFreeExplains how the app supports cravings, planning and progress.
  2. 3Consent & privacyCollects informed consent and explains data choices in plain language.
  3. 4Baseline assessmentRecords current smoking patterns without judgment.
  4. 5Trigger mapIdentifies situations and feelings linked to smoking.
  5. 6Readiness resultReflects readiness and recommends a flexible next step.

Phase 3 of 8 · 5 screens

Quit plan

A plan the person builds themselves: a path, a date, their own reasons, and one named supporter.

  1. 7Choose quit pathLets the patient choose a quit-date or gradual-reduction path.
  2. 8Select quit dateSelects a realistic quit date with planning guidance.
  3. 9My reasonsCaptures personal reasons the patient wants to quit.
  4. 10Support preparationChooses a supporter and a specific help phrase.
  5. 11Review quit planReviews every plan choice before activation.

Phase 4 of 8 · 5 screens

Daily support

A check-in that takes under ninety seconds, and one manageable next step chosen from the answer.

  1. 12Preparation homeDaily home before quit day with tasks, check-in and craving support.
  2. 13Daily check-inRecords a quick, nonpunitive daily status in under 90 seconds.
  3. 14Personalized next stepRecommends one manageable action based on the check-in.
  4. 15Guided stress resetRuns a calm, guided breathing exercise.
  5. 16Exercise complete & recheckMeasures how the exercise felt and recommends what to do next.

Phase 5 of 8 · 5 screens

Craving rescue

Immediate, one-handed help that works offline, followed by an honest recheck of how it went.

  1. 17Craving rescue startStarts immediate, one-handed help that also works offline.
  2. 18Recommended rescue toolSuggests a short coping tool matched to the craving.
  3. 19Active craving rescueGuides the selected coping tool in real time.
  4. 20Craving recheckRechecks craving intensity without claiming a clinical measurement.
  5. 21Rescue result & next stepShows the patient-reported change and offers another safe option.

Phase 6 of 8 · 2 screens

Quit day

Support through the hardest day, and a slip response that keeps every piece of prior progress.

  1. 22Quit-day homeSupports the patient through quit day with immediate help and check-ins.
  2. 23Slip recoveryResponds to a slip with support while preserving all prior progress.

Phase 7 of 8 · 4 screens

Resources

Medication education without prescribing, reviewed lessons, patterns without punishment, and real human support.

  1. 24Medication centerProvides education and self-tracking without prescribing.
  2. 25Learn libraryOffers clinically reviewed, plain-language learning resources.
  3. 26Progress dashboardShows patterns and estimates without punishment after slips.
  4. 27Support hubConnects the patient to quitlines, supporters, coaches and care teams.

Phase 8 of 8 · 1 screen

Account

Notification privacy, consent, export, accessibility and deletion — in one place, under the person's control.

  1. 28Settings & privacyCentralizes notification privacy, consent, exports, accessibility and deletion.

One more thing

Nobody has to do all of this.

Someone can open BreatheFree for the first time in the middle of a craving, use one two-minute breathing exercise, and close it again. The plan, the check-ins and the dashboard are there when they are wanted — not a queue to get through first.

Craving rescue is designed to work offline, one-handed, with no typing required.
