What we make

One system. Many areas of health.

The hard parts of behaviour change are the same whether someone is quitting cigarettes, managing blood glucose or keeping up with a screening schedule: a plan you actually believe in, help in the moment it is needed, and a record that survives a bad week.

Tether Health brings that together once — consent, plain-language education, coping tools, progress without punishment, and routes to real people — and shapes it to each health area.

A plan people own

Built from someone's own reasons, their own date, their own supporter.

Help in the moment

Short, guided tools for the minutes that decide the outcome.

Progress without punishment

Patterns and estimates that a setback never wipes out.

A route to a person

Quitlines, supporters, coaches and care teams — never a dead end.

How we build

Four commitments we will not trade away.

  • Private by design

    No ads. No selling health data. Sensitive notification text is off by default, so a lock screen never announces someone's health to the room.

  • No judgment, just support

    A setback never erases progress. Our tools are built to respond to a difficult day with help rather than a broken streak.

  • Works when it is needed

    The hardest moments do not wait, so core support tools are designed to work one-handed.

  • Designed to be usable

    Designed for plain language in English, screen readers and larger text.

Coming in the app

Bring your health records.

Importing your records is coming in the app. It is not available today. When it arrives it is opt-in only: you choose a source, review every item before it is added, and can disconnect at any time. It will use HL7® FHIR® and SMART on FHIR, the open standards US health records use.

How import will work

  • Epic MyChart

    Bring in records from a health system that uses Epic, through your own MyChart sign-in. Coming in the app.

  • Medicare Blue Button

    People with Medicare will be able to bring in their claims history by signing in with Medicare. Coming in the app.

  • Apple Health

    Import the records you keep in Apple Health, after your phone asks your permission. Coming in the app.

  • Health Connect

    Import the records you keep in Android's Health Connect, after your phone asks your permission. Coming in the app.

Work with your doctor

Designed to support the care you already have.

Tether Health does not diagnose, treat or prescribe, and it does not replace your clinician. It helps you bring what you track to the people who care for you, on your terms.

  • A visit summary you preview first

    Share a summary of what you have tracked before an appointment. You see exactly what it contains before it goes anywhere.

  • Connect a clinic with an invite code

    If your clinic gives you an invite code, you can connect to it from the app. Nothing is shared until you choose to.

  • Safety help, one tap away

    Emergency and crisis help, 911 and 988, is always one tap away in the app.
